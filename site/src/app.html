Get the app.
Android 10 or newer.

Download the APK

Install it

Open the downloaded file. Android asks once to allow installs from your browser. Play Protect may ask you to confirm: the app is not on Google Play yet.

Pair it

On your computer, tell your agent: set up my phone, reading mobile.orkestrate.space/phone/agent.md. Then scan the code it shows with this phone's camera.

Source and build notes are on GitHub. The app only connects to the relay you pair it with.
