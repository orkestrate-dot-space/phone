Your agent installs the relay as a user service and shows a QR code. No root.

Point the phone's camera at the code. The app opens and pairs. Without the app, the page offers it first.

Allow

Notifications carry approval cards. Background access keeps the link up when the screen is off.

Approve

Photos and commands wait for a tap on the phone. No call can approve itself.
